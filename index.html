WEBSITE HELPER

Open another website

↗

Open a website in a separate tab. Use that website's own upload and camera controls. Browser security prevents Bharul from controlling another tab or replacing its camera feed.

Bharul cannot approve permission prompts, bypass sign-in, or inject media into another website's live camera. You remain in control of camera and microphone permissions.
